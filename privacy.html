Privacy Policy

Operator: Gunoluj · Effective September 29, 2026

Gunoluj is a recipe organizer. Everything you enter stays on this device. Gunoluj has no accounts on a server, no cloud sync and no advertising SDKs.

1. Information you provide

  • Recipe text you paste, type or dictate, and the ingredients, amounts, units and steps you proofread.
  • Recipe images you capture with Take Recipe Photo or import with Choose Photo. A working copy is stored in the app's private folder. Your original photo in the Photos library is never modified.
  • Cooking selections, shopping list check marks and timer values you set.
  • Cooking packs (PDF files) you choose to generate.

2. How information is processed

Text recognition from images uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Speech transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition and only starts when on-device recognition is available. Recipe parsing and shopping list merging run on this device with fixed rules; Gunoluj does not send your recipes to any third party service or AI provider.

3. Permissions

  • Camera — only when you tap Take Recipe Photo, to capture recipe text you already have.
  • Photos — only when you tap Choose Photo, to import a recipe image that contains text.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — only when you tap Speak Recipe Text, to transcribe a recipe you read aloud.
  • Notifications — used to tell you when a kitchen timer you started has finished.
  • App Tracking Transparency — Gunoluj shows Apple's system prompt; Gunoluj does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you decline a permission, you can still type or paste recipe text.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Gunoluj receives a signed transaction record from Apple (transaction ID, product ID and date) and stores it on this device to add Sparks to your balance exactly once. Gunoluj never sees your payment details. Consumable Sparks are stored on this device and cannot be restored after the app is removed or its data is cleared.

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored in the app's private storage on this device until you delete it. Deleting a recipe does not delete cooking packs you already generated.

6. Log Out, Clear All Data and Delete Account

  • Log Out ends the local session. Your recipes, packs and Sparks stay on this device.
  • Clear All Data permanently erases recipes, drafts, media, cooking packs, purchase records, preferences and remaining Sparks, then returns you to the first-run screen.
  • Delete Account performs the same erase and also ends the local session.

7. Children

Gunoluj is a general audience cooking utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be bundled with the app update and the effective date above will change.
