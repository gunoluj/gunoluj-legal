Support

Questions about Gunoluj? Email support@gunoluj.app.

1Text was not recognized from my photo

Gunoluj reads recipe text only. Photos of finished dishes do not contain a recipe; paste or type the recipe text instead.

2Why are some ingredients listed separately?

Items are combined only when the name and unit are exactly the same. Different units and missing amounts stay on their own lines so nothing is guessed.

3Did I lose Sparks when generation failed?

No. Sparks are used only after the pack PDF is saved.

4Can I restore Sparks on a new phone?

No. Sparks are consumable and stored on this device.

5I denied camera, photos or microphone

You can still paste text, or enable access in the iOS Settings app.
