Support

Questions about Gunoluj? Email support@gunoluj.app.

Common questions

  • Text was not recognized from my photo. Gunoluj reads recipe text only. Photos of finished dishes do not contain a recipe; paste or type the recipe text instead.
  • Why are some ingredients listed separately? Items are merged only when the name and unit match exactly. Different units and missing amounts are kept separate so nothing is guessed.
  • Did I lose Sparks when generation failed? No. Sparks are used only after the pack PDF is saved.
  • Can I restore Sparks on a new phone? No. Sparks are consumable and stored on this device.
  • I denied camera, photos or microphone. You can still paste text, or enable access in the iOS Settings app.
